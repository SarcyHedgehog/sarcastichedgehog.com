How to play

Clear yours. Flood theirs.

  1. Tap letters to build one or more words.
  2. Choose a minimum word length before battle. Each letter beyond it sends one attack tile, up to four.
  3. Pink removes a tile, gold moves a tile, and paired red tiles earn a Spy Glass.
  4. Pink, gold and red special tiles enter play from turns 3, 5 and 7 respectively.
  5. At the start of your turn, attacks land first, followed by fresh letters.
  6. A tile touching the overflow line loses. Every 14 player-turns, that line drops one row across both towers.
  7. Empty your board, or make your opponent hit the line, to win.

For this prototype, all combinations meeting the chosen minimum length count as words.
